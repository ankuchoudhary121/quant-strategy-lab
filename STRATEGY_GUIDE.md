# Strategy Guide

Quant Strategy Lab includes six core strategies:

- **Moving Average Crossover**: Uses a short- and long-term moving average to identify trend changes.
- **RSI Mean Reversion**: Uses the Relative Strength Index to identify oversold and overbought conditions.
- **MACD Trend Following**: Uses MACD and its signal line to identify trend direction and momentum.
- **Bollinger Mean Reversion**: Uses Bollinger Bands to identify prices that have moved unusually far from their rolling average.
- **Donchian Channel Breakout**: Uses rolling price highs and lows to identify breakouts.
- **Buy and Hold**: Holds the selected assets as a passive baseline for comparison.

The Strategy Comparison tab runs these six strategies on the same price data and backtest settings.
